Job Finder — Privacy Policy Effective date: 31 August 2026 Job Finder ("the app") is a personal application that runs locally on the computer of its individual owner and operator ("you"). This policy explains how the app handles data. Who runs the app The app is operated solely by its owner for personal use. There is no company, no shared server, and no other users. Contact: sleman.hab@gmail.com. What data the app accesses With your explicit Google sign-in consent, the app uses the Gmail API with the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify scope to read the content, headers, and labels of messages in your own Gmail account and to add or remove labels on those messages. The app does not send email, does not delete email, and does not change message content. It accesses only the signed-in owner's own mailbox. How the data is used The app processes your email locally on your own device to identify and label job-related messages. To classify messages it may send the relevant message text to (a) Google's Gmail API, which is the source of the data, and (b) an AI processing endpoint that you configure and choose. No email data is sent to any other destination. Where data is stored and sent The app runs entirely on your own computer. Your Google credentials (OAuth tokens) and any working data are stored locally on that computer. No email content, credentials, or personal data is transmitted to the developer, to any third-party server operated by the app, or to advertisers. The only external services that receive data are the Gmail API and the AI endpoint you yourself select, each used only to provide the app's core functionality to you. Sharing and selling The app does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. There are no third parties other than the service providers named above, which act only to deliver the feature you requested. Retention and deletion Data lives only on your local device for as long as you keep it. You can delete all stored data at any time by removing the app's local files and revoking the app's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Google API Services Limited Use disclosure Job Finder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide and improve the app's user-facing features, is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide those features, is not used for advertising, and is not used or transferred for purposes unrelated to the app's core functionality. Changes Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
